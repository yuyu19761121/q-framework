# Q-Framework

**Heterogeneous AI Compute Continuity**

Q-Framework develops new opportunities for coherent AI execution across independent, heterogeneous compute resources. Its public engineering record includes controlled compute-continuation results, completed AI video artifacts, and independently checked consistency outcomes.

### Selected results

- Cross-GPU controlled continuation with bitwise-identical output in the measured test.
- Completed MiniMax H3 video output following cross-worker execution.
- Four independent RTX 2080 Ti 22 GB workers contributing to a 15-second, 540-frame output.
- Controlled approximately 250 MiB compute-state transport with matching SHA-256.
- Three-environment recomputation consistency.
- QSTATIC and QmRNA comparison with 22 / 22 matching decoded frames.

### Technology vision

Possible applications span creator tools, professional video production, enterprise and workstation AI, edge computing, multi-hardware ecosystems, and commercial integrations. These are independent areas of opportunity.
