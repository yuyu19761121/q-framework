# Sponsor Q-Framework

Q-Framework is an experimental compute architecture exploring **compute-state virtualization, persistent execution state, adaptive pressure control, and heterogeneous AI infrastructure**.

This is **not a concept-only project**. The project already has a running development system, public architecture documentation, claim/evidence tracking, benchmark methodology, reference code, and multilingual whitepapers.

## Why sponsorship matters

Sponsorship helps turn a working research system into a more reproducible and externally verifiable engineering platform.

Priority uses include:

- additional GPU and heterogeneous hardware validation;
- NVMe, storage, networking, power and reliability infrastructure;
- runtime instrumentation and state-lifecycle engineering;
- repeatable benchmark and regression infrastructure;
- public evidence, multilingual documentation and security/IP review.

## Suggested sponsorship levels

| Level | Suggested contribution | Typical benefits |
|---|---:|---|
| Research Supporter | US$5,000+ | Sponsor listing (or anonymous), release updates, quarterly technical summary |
| Development Sponsor | US$25,000+ | Above + sponsor briefing, roadmap review, non-exclusive benchmark summaries |
| Infrastructure Sponsor | US$100,000+ | Above + priority validation on agreed hardware/workloads, potential joint case study |
| Founding Strategic Sponsor | US$250,000+ | Above + annual deep technical review, jointly defined validation program, strategic collaboration framework |

These are **sponsorship / research collaboration levels**, not equity, tokens, revenue share, securities, or investment-return commitments.

## What sponsors do not automatically receive

Sponsorship does not automatically grant:

- production source code;
- proprietary QmRNA control algorithms;
- Qvram production serialization / restore sequencing;
- Qsearch ranking or cache heuristics;
- scheduler weights, lease/fencing internals or retry authority;
- patents, trademarks, exclusive licensing or customer data.

Commercial licensing, OEM, custom development, exclusive rights or IP transfer require a separate agreement and scope of work.
