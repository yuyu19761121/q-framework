# QSTATIC | Multi-worker Visual Consistency

QSTATIC is one of the Q-Framework technology concepts associated with coordinated generative-media execution.

## Demonstrated result

A controlled multi-worker experiment involving four independent GPU resources produced consistent shared-reference-state verification results. The work also established a basis for producing different media actions within a coordinated multi-worker environment.

## Creative applications

The broader technology landscape includes character-based video production, parallel media creation, visual composition, creative workflows, and collaborative generative-media systems.
