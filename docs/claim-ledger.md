# Q-Framework | Publicly Demonstrated Outcomes

The following statements summarize selected engineering observations from completed controlled evaluations.

## Compute continuation

A controlled cross-GPU continuation test produced a bitwise-identical result with a measured maximum absolute difference of **0.0**.

## Media output

A MiniMax H3 workload completed video output following cross-worker continuation.

## Multi-worker collaboration

Four independent RTX 2080 Ti 22 GB GPU workers participated in the production of a **15-second, 540-frame** media artifact.

## State integrity

A controlled computation-state transport of approximately **250 MiB** preserved matching SHA-256 results.

## Cross-environment consistency

Three execution environments produced matching recomputation-result hashes for a numerical task derived from a completed workload.

## Visual consistency

A controlled QSTATIC and QmRNA comparison recorded identical decoded-frame hashes for **22 of 22 frames**.

The figures refer to their individually recorded evaluation conditions.
