# Q-Framework | Public Architecture Overview

Q-Framework brings coherent AI workload execution to a setting where multiple independent compute resources can cooperate.

```text
        AI WORKLOAD
             |
             v
       Q-FRAMEWORK
             |
             v
  HETEROGENEOUS COMPUTE
             |
             v
     COMPLETED OUTCOMES
```

## Engineering focus

Publicly demonstrated outcomes include coordinated GPU execution, verified computation-state transport, numerical consistency across execution environments, and completed generative-video production.

## Application landscape

Potential applications encompass content creation, professional AI services, workstation computing, edge environments, infrastructure collaboration, and research partnerships.
